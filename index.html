Bolo for your computer

Speak on your phone, and Bolo types it into whatever app is open on your computer.

Open this link on your computer

Bolo for computers installs on a Windows PC or a Mac, not on this phone. On the computer, open:

tinyurl.com/bolo-pc
  1. Open the link on your computer. The right download starts by itself.
  2. Install it. Bolo then shows a QR code on the computer.
  3. On this phone: open the Bolo app, go to PC Mode and scan that QR code. Your phone and computer are now paired.
