Bolo for your computer

Speak on your phone, and Bolo types it into whatever app is open on your computer.

You are on a phone. Open tinyurl.com/bolo-pc on the computer you want Bolo on, or pick it below and send yourself the link.
Download for Windowsabout 27 MB
  1. Open the downloaded zip and double-click Bolo-Server.exe.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Bolo installs itself and sits in the system tray (bottom right). A QR code opens.
  4. On your phone: open Bolo, go to PC Mode and scan the QR code.
